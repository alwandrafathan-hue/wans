# Prompt Storyboard Komik · Lahan Pak Karto (versi 10 menit)

Prompt untuk ChatGPT (pembuat gambar) agar naskah menjadi storyboard bergaya komik. Prompt ditulis dalam bahasa Inggris karena hasil gambarnya lebih akurat. Dialog di balon kata tetap berbahasa Indonesia.

## Cara pakai

1. Buka satu percakapan baru di ChatGPT dan pakai percakapan yang sama sampai selesai, supaya wajah tokoh tetap konsisten.
2. Tempel **Prompt 0 (lembar karakter)** lebih dulu. Simpan hasilnya.
3. Tempel **Halaman 1** sampai **Halaman 8** satu per satu. Kalau wajah tokoh mulai berubah, unggah ulang gambar lembar karakter lalu tulis: *"Use the characters exactly as in this reference sheet."*
4. Kalau tulisan di balon kata salah eja, minta: *"Same image, but leave all speech bubbles empty."* Lalu isi teksnya sendiri di Canva atau PowerPoint.
5. **Logo jangan dibuat oleh AI.** AI akan mengubah bentuk logo, padahal pedoman Danantara melarang itu. Semua prompt meminta ruang kosong; tempel logo asli saat menyunting.

## Aturan yang sudah dimasukkan ke setiap prompt

- Gaya warna datar ala cetak dengan palet acara: krem `#F2EADA`, navy `#14213D`, kuning `#FBC800`, terakota `#E4572E`.
- Wajah Pak Karto tidak terlihat sampai Halaman 7 (adegan 13).
- Adegan K3 selalu memakai APD lengkap. Tidak ada kecelakaan dan tidak ada pestisida.
- Tokohnya hanya taruna dan petani.

## Prompt 0 · Lembar karakter

```
Create a character reference sheet for an original Indonesian comic, 4 characters standing side by side on a plain cream background (#F2EADA), full body, front view, with a small face close-up above each one and the name written under each.

Art style: clean modern comic / graphic novel, confident ink outlines, flat print colors with no gradients, no neon, no glossy effects. Limited palette: cream #F2EADA, navy #14213D, warm yellow #FBC800, terracotta #E4572E, plus natural skin tones and muted earth browns.

Characters (all Indonesian, all original, not based on real people):
1. RAKA, 21, male agronomy trainee. Tall, neat short black hair with a side part, sharp eyebrows, serious face. White training shirt, navy trousers, black marker pen clipped to his chest pocket.
2. DIMAS, 21, male agronomy trainee. Stocky, curly black hair, wide friendly grin. White training shirt, navy trousers, holding a tablet.
3. SARI, 22, female agronomy trainee. Shoulder-length black hair in a ponytail, thin rectangular glasses, confident and a little cold. White training shirt, navy trousers.
4. NADIA, 20, female agronomy trainee. Small and quiet, wears a simple cream hijab, gentle eyes, holds a small notebook to her chest. White training shirt, navy trousers.

Below them, a fifth figure: PAK KARTO, 63, Indonesian rice farmer, thin, sun-darkened skin, grey mustache, faded brown long-sleeve shirt, rolled trousers, a woven bamboo conical hat (caping). Show him only from the back and as a silhouette, his face is never shown on this sheet.

No logos, no brand names, no extra text besides the names.
```
