# Prompt Storyboard Komik · Lahan Pak Karto (alur film mata-mata, 10 menit)

Prompt untuk ChatGPT (pembuat gambar) agar naskah menjadi storyboard bergaya komik. Prompt ditulis dalam bahasa Inggris karena hasil gambarnya lebih akurat. Dialog di balon kata tetap berbahasa Indonesia.

## Yang berlaku di versi ini

- **Latar hanya lokasi induksi** (rundown 12–16 Desember): mess, lapangan Binsik, ruang rapat, Mobil Uji Tanah, ruang komputer, kelas, area outbond, halaman diklat, ruang makan, dan Wisma Kebomas saat Wirakarya 17 Desember. **Tidak ada adegan di sawah.**
- **Tokoh: enam taruna dari enam kampus dan Pak Ilham** (VP Pengelolaan Pelanggan) sebagai mentor pemberi misi.
- **Pak Karto tidak pernah digambar.** Ia hanya muncul sebagai pesan teks di layar ponsel, dan satu foto tangan yang memegang nampan bibit.
- Alur film mata-mata: transmisi masuk, perekrutan dengan nama sandi, pemberian misi, ujian, misi gagal, suit-up, operasi, misi berhasil, agen diterjunkan.

## Cara pakai

1. Buka satu percakapan baru di ChatGPT dan pakai percakapan yang sama sampai selesai, supaya wajah tokoh konsisten.
2. Tempel **Prompt 0 (berkas agen)** lebih dulu. Simpan hasilnya.
3. Tempel **Halaman 1** sampai **Halaman 8** satu per satu. Kalau wajah tokoh berubah, unggah ulang gambar Prompt 0 lalu tulis: *"Use the characters exactly as in this reference sheet."*
4. Kalau tulisan di balon kata salah eja, minta: *"Same image, but leave all speech bubbles and captions empty."* Lalu isi teksnya sendiri di Canva atau PowerPoint.
5. **Pak Ilham digambar sebagai tokoh umum, bukan wajah aslinya.** ChatGPT umumnya menolak menggambar wajah orang sungguhan. Storyboard hanya untuk gambaran shot. Saat syuting, beliau tampil sebagai dirinya sendiri.
6. **Logo jangan dibuat oleh AI.** Semua prompt meminta ruang kosong; tempel logo asli saat menyunting.

## Prompt 0 · Berkas agen (lembar karakter)

```
Create a character reference sheet designed like a classified spy-agency personnel file, for an original Indonesian comic. Six agent profile cards in two rows of three on a navy background (#14213D), plus one larger mentor card on the right. Each card: cream paper (#F2EADA), a full-body drawing, a face close-up in a square photo frame, the name, a small campus label, and a codename in a yellow (#FBC800) tag.

Art style: sleek modern spy-comic / graphic novel, confident ink outlines, flat print colors, no gradients, no neon, no glossy effects. Sharp, symmetrical composition. Limited palette: cream #F2EADA, navy #14213D, yellow #FBC800, terracotta #E4572E, plus natural skin tones.

All characters are original Indonesian characters, not based on real people or existing films. All six trainees wear a crisp white training shirt and navy trousers.
1. RAKA, codename "KOMPAS", campus "Yogyakarta-Magelang". 21, male, tall, neat short black hair with a side part, serious leader face, black marker pen in his chest pocket.
2. SARI, codename "TABUNG", campus "Malang". 22, female, black hair in a ponytail, thin rectangular glasses, confident and a little cold.
3. DIMAS, codename "ELANG", campus "Bogor". 21, male, stocky, curly black hair, wide friendly grin, holding a tablet with a satellite map.
4. BONA, codename "JAM", campus "Medan". 21, male, broad shoulders, short buzz cut, straight direct expression, a big sports wristwatch he keeps checking.
5. LAKSMI, codename "PERISAI", campus "LPP Yogyakarta". 21, female, hair in a neat low bun, calm careful eyes, holding a small safety checklist.
6. NADIA, codename "AKAR", campus "Gowa". 20, female, small and quiet, simple cream hijab, gentle eyes, holding a small notebook to her chest.

Mentor card (larger, labeled "MENTOR"): a senior Indonesian executive around 50 in a dark navy suit and white shirt, calm and composed, slight knowing smile. This is a generic illustrated character, not a likeness of any real person. Label him only "PAK ILHAM · VP PENGELOLAAN PELANGGAN".

No farmer, no rice fields. No weapons. No logos, no extra text besides names, campus labels, codenames and the mentor label.
```
